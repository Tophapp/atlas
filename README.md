The object of the game is to get the amount of people you need to the base, or the white box, without being defeated by the enemy team.  The player's armies are represented by the green boxes, and the enemies' are represented by the red boxes.  If you get too close to an enemy with an army, it'll go after it and attack it, but this detection range depends on the size of the army.  Right click to break an army apart in half, and if two armies get close, they combine to form a larger one.
